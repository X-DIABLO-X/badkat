How it works

01

It watches the foreground

Every second it reads the window in front of you — the URL, the title and the process. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is logged; the check happens on your machine and the answer is thrown away.

02

Each site gets a leash

Shorts and Reels get six seconds. Instagram proper gets forty-five. Ordinary YouTube gets four minutes, because plenty of YouTube is work. Every rule and every timer is yours to change.

03

Then it comes over

The cat walks to the offending window, plants itself, and counts down. Click it during the countdown and it relents. Ignore it and the paw lands — Ctrl+W for a tab, a close for anything else.

It remembers what you did together

Every window it closes earns XP, and so does patting it. Levels cost more as they go, so the number means something by the time it gets high. Level-ups unlock new lines and animations.

  • 25 XP per window actually closed
  • 5 XP for a pat, rate limited so it stays honest
  • Progress is saved separately from your settings and survives a reset

Get it

Windows 10 and 11. It installs to your user folder, so it needs no admin rights, and it updates itself from inside the app — you will not have to come back here.
