What counts

Each rule is matched against the URL, the window title and the process name of whatever is in front of you. First match wins, so put specific rules above general ones.

A browser only ever exposes the site's own title, so Instagram Reels reads as just “Instagram” and a YouTube Short as “<name> - YouTube”. The word reels or shorts exists only in the URL. But a browser playing fullscreen video drops its address bar entirely, so the URL goes blank exactly while you watch a film. That is why both are matched, and why some rules use a domain and others a title fragment.
